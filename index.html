About This Tool

Image Color Inspector is a free online image color palette extractor that runs fully in your browser. You can upload an image, reduce it to N representative colors, inspect HEX values by pixel coordinate, and export the palette as CSV or Adobe ACO.

The app also supports optional background removal before quantization, with model selection and threshold control for better foreground-focused palettes.

FAQ

Q: Can I use it for transparent PNGs? A: Yes. Transparent regions are ignored during quantization so foreground colors remain dominant.

Q: Does it upload images to a backend? A: No dedicated backend is required for processing; it is frontend-first and runs inference in-browser.
